BatoBatoPick

Bato Bato Pick is an interactive web-based mini-game where players go head-to-head against a computer bot in the classic hand game of Rock, Paper, and Scissors. How to play: Choose your move by clicking either Rock, Paper, or Scissors, and the system will instantly generate the bot's choice to determine the winner.

As the lead developer and programmer, I designed and implemented the core game logic, interactive user interface elements, and bot decision-making mechanics that power the Bato Bato Pick experience.
